  * "HIGH": Blocked accessibility, active resource waste running >2 hours, or vendor compliance breach.
  * "MEDIUM": Localized operational inefficiency or recurring non-compliance with no active damage.
  * "LOW": Informational suggestions, visual defects, or routine inquiries.
- recommended_team: Choose EXACTLY one:
  ["Facilities Rapid Response", "Sustainability Operations", "Procurement Compliance", "Accessibility & Inclusion", "Corporate Legal"]
- followup_required: true or false (Boolean).
- followup_action: Specific immediate task required. If none, return "None".
- data_sensitivity_risk: Choose EXACTLY one:
  ["CONFIDENTIAL_WHISTLEBLOWER", "PII_PRESENT", "STANDARD_OPERATIONAL"]
- escalation_reason: Operational justification if HIGH or CRITICAL; otherwise return "N/A" (max 15 words).
- brief_summary: Objective description of the event (max 20 words).
